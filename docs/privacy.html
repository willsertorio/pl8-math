pl8(math)

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

In short: pl8(math) has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your gyms, PRs and workouts stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. A photo or its text only leaves your phone if your phone can't read it itself, and only after you say yes. Nothing is kept after it's read. We collect anonymous usage stats to improve the app.

Who we are

pl8(math) is made by Will Sertorio, an individual developer based in Brazil ("we", "us"). You can reach us at williansertorio@gmail.com.

What stays on your device and in your iCloud

The things you set up in the app stay on your iPhone: your gym's name, units, plates and bar, your PRs, and your workouts and finished sets. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also synced to your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so they follow you to a new iPhone. We can't see or access this data. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

We never store your photos. The app keeps the text it read from them and what it found in that text, for example "Back squat 5×5 @ 75%". Like the rest of your data, this stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud.

Reading photos of your workout or PRs

When you upload a photo of a whiteboard or a PR list, your iPhone first tries to read it on the device. Most of the time, nothing leaves your phone.

If your phone can't read it, the app asks your permission before using the cloud. If you allow it, the app sends the text it recognised to our server. It sends the photo itself only when no text could be recognised, and it removes location and other metadata first. Our server passes it to an AI service, Claude by Anthropic, which returns the workout or PRs and nothing else.

  • Nothing you send is stored by us after the result is returned, and we don't log its contents.
  • To make sure only pl8(math) can use our server, your iPhone proves it's running the real app (Apple's App Attest). For that, our server keeps a random key your iPhone made for this app and a count of reads per day, to stop abuse. The key isn't linked to you, your Apple Account or your data.
  • Under its commercial API terms, Anthropic does not use this content to train its models.
  • You can turn the cloud option off at any time in the app. The app then uses only on-device reading or manual entry.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn what works and what doesn't, the app sends anonymous events to PostHog, our analytics provider, hosted in the EU. Examples are "finished onboarding", "a photo was read on the device or in the cloud" and "a session was finished".

  • Events are tied to a random identifier created by the app, not to you. We don't know your name, email or Apple Account.
  • We don't send your photos, the text in them, your gym name or your PR numbers.
  • IP addresses are discarded, and there's no session recording.

Crash reports may also reach us through Apple if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data with data brokers.

Your choices and rights

You're in control of your data:

  • Delete everything: delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage and remove pl8(math)'s data.
  • Cloud reading: turn it off in the app at any time.
  • Analytics: because events aren't linked to you, we can't look them up by person. If you have a question or a request (access, correction, deletion, objection) under Brazil's LGPD, the EU's GDPR or a similar law, email us and we'll help. You can also complain to Brazil's data protection authority (ANPD), or to the data protection authority where you live, such as the AEPD in Spain.

Our legal bases are your consent for cloud reading, and our legitimate interest in improving the app for the anonymous statistics. Our server and analytics run in the EU, and the AI service may process content in the United States. Where data leaves Brazil or the EU, we rely on our providers' standard safeguards for international transfers.

Children

pl8(math) is a training tool for adults and isn't directed at children under 16. We don't knowingly process children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
