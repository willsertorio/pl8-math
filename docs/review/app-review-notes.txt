2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
pl8(math) is a plate calculator for CrossFit athletes and Olympic weightlifters. Box workouts are written as percentages of a lifter's one-rep max (e.g. "Back squat 5x5 @ 75%"). Today lifters work out each set on a calculator mid-class: percentage of their PR, convert kilos/pounds, subtract the bar, split into plates. pl8(math) reads a screenshot or photo of the workout and shows the plates to take from the rack for the whole session, then guides the lifter set by set: what to add or take off, plus a warm-up.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login needed)
- Launch the app and tap the title.
- Answer the setup questions: gym name, kilos or pounds, the plates your gym has, your bar.
- PRs: type them in, or upload a PR screenshot. Sample: https://willsertorio.github.io/pl8-math/review/sample-prs.png
- Workout: upload a workout screenshot on the whiteboard step. Sample: https://willsertorio.github.io/pl8-math/review/sample-workout.png (save both to Photos first).
- The app shows the plate kit, then each set. Tap through the sets and end the session.
- The menu lets you edit gyms, PRs and the workout. A photo the app can't read shows question marks on the board.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple Vision (on device): reads the text in the photo.
- Apple Foundation Models (on device, iOS 26 devices with Apple Intelligence): reads the text into lifts when the built-in rules can't.
- pl8(math) read server (Vercel, EU region) with Anthropic's Claude API: only when the phone can't read a photo, and only after the user agrees in an alert (switchable in Settings > pl8(math)). It receives the recognized text (or the photo when no text was found) and returns the lifts; nothing is stored. Access is limited to the genuine app with Apple App Attest; Upstash Redis (EU) stores only the App Attest keys and a daily read count.
- iCloud / CloudKit: syncs the user's gyms, PRs and workouts to their private iCloud database.
- PostHog (EU): anonymous usage analytics, no personal data, not linked to the user, no tracking.

5. REGIONS
The app works the same in every region. The on-device model is used only where Apple Intelligence is available; elsewhere the app uses its rules and, with consent, the cloud reading.
