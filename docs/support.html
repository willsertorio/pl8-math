Support

Help with the app

Need help? Email williansertorio@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model and iOS version, and add a screenshot if you can. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

A photo wasn't read (question marks on the board). Try a sharper, straighter photo or a screenshot from your box's app, with the lifts and percentages in view. If your phone can't read it, the app can ask to read it in the cloud; you can also enter the workout by hand.

Turn cloud reading on or off. In your iPhone's Settings → pl8(math) → Read in the cloud.

My data on a new iPhone. Sign in to the same iCloud account; your gyms, PRs and workouts sync through your private iCloud.

Delete my data. Delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage and remove pl8(math)'s data.

The plates look wrong. Check your gym's bar weight and the plates you have in the gym menu. If it's still wrong, email us the workout screenshot and what you expected.
